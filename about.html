How we work

Honest by design

The challenge

Dancing with the SARs, NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026.

Why mangroves first

Mangroves are a coastal ecosystem under pressure, and radar is well suited to watch them through the clouds of the Caribbean coast.

How we use AI

An AI language model only turns our computed results into plain language. It never sees raw satellite data and cannot add numbers. Every explanation is labeled.

Data credits

NISAR data: NASA and ISRO, distributed by the Alaska Satellite Facility DAAC. Processing, maps and results: HTTP Polaris.

Images

Banner landscapes are AI-generated illustrations. Ciénaga Grande photos belong to their original authors. Radar images are real NISAR scenes colored by our code.

Open source

The code is open. See the repository.
