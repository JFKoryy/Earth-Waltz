About

About Earth Waltz

Earth Waltz is a website that turns NISAR radar data into stories about our changing planet. We built it so anyone can see how a landscape changes, and understand why, in plain language.

The team

HTTP Polaris. [Member name · role] [Member name · role]

The challenge

Dancing with the SARs, NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026.

Why mangroves first

Mangroves are a coastal ecosystem under pressure, and radar is well suited to watch them through the clouds of the Caribbean coast.

How we use AI

We use an AI language model only to turn computed results into plain-language explanations. The text is generated in advance, from our own results file, and labeled “AI-generated, based on computed data”. The AI never sees raw satellite data and cannot add numbers. [Model name and review process]
