Radar sees through clouds

NISAR sends its own microwave pulses, so it can image Earth day or night, in clear skies or under thick clouds. Where cameras go blind, radar keeps watching.

AI explains, data decides

Our code measures the change. The AI only translates those numbers into plain language, so it can never invent a result.

Open and free

NISAR data is open to everyone. So is this website: you can check our sources, our method and our code.
