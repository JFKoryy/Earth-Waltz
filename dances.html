EARTHWALTZ

Turning radar data into stories about our changing planet.

Built by team HTTP Polaris (Andrea Valentina Granados García & Jesús Múnera) for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 · Challenge: Dancing with the SARs.

Data & AI

Contains data from NISAR (NASA-ISRO Synthetic Aperture Radar), openly available through the Alaska Satellite Facility DAAC. Data processing and results by the HTTP Polaris team.

Explanations marked “AI-generated” were written by an AI model from computed results. Reviewed by the team.
